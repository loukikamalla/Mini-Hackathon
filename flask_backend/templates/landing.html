DHANYA

Automated Rice Mill & Civil Supplies Digital Reconciliation System (CMR 67%)

DHANYA is an automated statutory governance platform designed for the Food & Civil Supplies Department and Rice Mill Operators. It eliminates manual register discrepancies between Mandi procurement dispatches and Mill weighbridge intakes, enforces statutory 67% CMR raw rice out-turn compliance, and automates ₹25.00/Qtl milling remuneration and farmer DBT payouts.

⚖️

Statutory 67% CMR Out-Turn & ₹25/Qtl Remuneration

Enforces mandatory 67% conversion (100 Qtl Paddy = 67 Qtl CMR Rice) and calculates statutory remuneration (₹10 milling + ₹4.50 handling + ₹4.40 gunny + ₹6.10 delivery incentive = ₹25.00/Qtl).

🔍

Real-Time ±1% Scale & 17% Moisture Discrepancy Engine

Automatically cross-matches vehicle numbers, flags net scale variances exceeding ±1.0%, and detects excessive moisture beyond the 17.0% threshold.

📜

Digital JRC with Verifiable QR Code & Farmer DBT Gateway

Issues official downloadable Joint Reconciliation Certificates (JRC) with dynamic officer digital signatures, PFMS clearance tokens, QR code verification, and simulated SMS DBT alerts.
