How DHANYA Automates CMR Governance

01

Multi-Source Data Ingestion

Ingests official Mandi manifests from Telangana OPMS, Excel statements, handwritten notebook photo OCR scans, and manual weighbridge slips.

02

Pandas Discrepancy Engine

Automates vehicle and transit pass cross-matching. Categorizes batches into Matches vs Mismatches with moisture (>17%) and tare variance reasons.

03

Statutory JRC & DBT Settlement

Computes 67% CMR raw rice target, milling (₹10/Qtl), handling (₹4.50/Qtl) and gunny credits. Authorizes digital JRC certificate and DBT SMS alerts.
