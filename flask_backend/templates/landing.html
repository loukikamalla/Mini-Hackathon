DHANYA

Paddy Procurement & CMR 67% Digital Reconciliation Platform

DHANYA is an automated governance system designed for the Food & Civil Supplies Department and Rice Mill Operators. It bridges the gap between Government Mandi Procurement manifests and Rice Mill Inward Weighbridge records, eliminating register discrepancies and accelerating statutory subsidy clearance.

⚖️

Statutory 67% CMR Out-Turn Compliance

Automates mandatory Custom Milling conversion (100 Qtl Paddy = 67 Qtl CMR Raw Rice) and calculates milling subsidy (₹10/Qtl) & handling fees (₹4.50/Qtl).

🔍

Automated Discrepancy & Moisture Detection

Cross-verifies vehicle numbers, gross/tare scale variations, and automatically flags moisture penalties exceeding the 17.0% threshold.

📜

Digital JRC Authorization & Farmer DBT Alerts

Issues official Joint Reconciliation Certificates with dynamic officer signatures and dispatches instant Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) SMS alerts.
